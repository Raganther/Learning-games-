Learning Games

Browser games for English learners — no login, no install.

ESL Connections

Find four groups of four words linked by collocations, phrasal verbs, spelling or register. Every solved group unlocks a mini-lesson. Five puzzles, A2–B2.

Story Reveal

A short story is hiding behind black blocks. Guess words to uncover it and name the topic. Trains reading and prediction. Three stories, A2–B2.

Sound Sprint

Hear a word, tap the one you heard: ship or sheep? berry or very? Twenty fast rounds with streaks, then a report on which sounds to practice.

Grammar Casino

Bet chips on whether a sentence is correct English. Bet big only when you're sure — every round ends with a one-line lesson. Ten sentences, B1–B2.

Dialogue Detangle

A real conversation got shuffled. Rebuild it line by line in a chat-app interface — then see why it flows. Trains pragmatics and discourse. Three dialogues, A2–B2.

Emoji Idioms

🍰👌 = a piece of cake. Ten emoji riddles hide English idioms — type them, take hints if you must, learn the meaning and an example every round.

Lost in London

One day in the city, six conversations, three ways to say everything. Choose your words, watch your rapport with the locals rise or fall, and leave with a phrasebook.

Word Forge

The customer wants "a word meaning not able to be broken". Hammer prefixes, roots and suffixes together on the anvil and forge it. Trains word-building.

The Alibi

A noir detective game where the suspect's tenses hide the true order of events. "The party had already started…" — reconstruct the real timeline. Trains tense comprehension.

Word Bridges

FIRE → ? → ? → KEEPER. Every neighbouring pair forms a compound word — fill the missing spans to complete the bridge. Trains compound nouns.

The Bouncer

You work the door at Club WORD. Real spellings get in; fakes like "recieve" and "tommorow" go home. Twenty rapid calls, streaks, and a corrections list at closing time.

Word Thermometer

English comes in degrees: cool → warm → hot → boiling. Load gradable words into the thermometer in order of intensity, and learn which extremes take "absolutely".

Red Pen

You're the editor. Each manuscript has exactly one wrong word — or none. Click the culprit or stamp it approved. Trains proofreading and classic error awareness.

Close Words

The Contexto-style game, tuned for learners: guess the secret word and every guess comes back with a temperature — 🔥 burning, 🌤️ warm, ❄️ cool, 🧊 cold. Win and harvest the word's whole neighbourhood.

Double Agents

One word, two identities: "a place that keeps your money" + "the side of a river". Read both dossiers and unmask the agent. Teaches words with double meanings.

Perfect Partners

A memory-flip game where matches are natural word pairs: heavy+rain, catch+a cold, take+a photo. Wrong flips turn back over — remember where the words live.
